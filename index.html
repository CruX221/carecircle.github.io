CareCompanion AI

Autonomous AI Caregiver for Chronic Disease Management

3️⃣ The Gap: Solitary seniors and aging parents living away from adult children face unmonitored medical risks and treatment fatigue.
💡 The Innovation: Outbound PSTN voice calls in cloned familiar voices, wearable biometrics, and automated e-pharmacy wallet fulfillment.

The Problem: Solitary Chronic Care Crisis

50%+ Non-Adherence

Chronic disease patients routinely skip medication, diet, or physiotherapy routines due to isolation, fatigue, or cognitive decline.

Unseen Biometric Anomalies

Gradual drops in blood oxygen, glucose spikes, or sleep disruption go unnoticed until they lead to acute hospitalizations.

Friction in Fulfillment

Elderly patients struggle to navigate smartphone apps to order refills, causing frequent medication stockouts.

Geographic Caregiver Distance

Adult children living in other cities or countries lack real-time visibility and proactive emergency intervention tools.

The Solution: 24/7 Digital Caregiver

🗣️ Voice-First Engagement

Autonomous PSTN calls in regional languages using cloned voices of loved ones. No smartphone app navigation required.

⌚ Wearable Fusion

Continuous data streaming from Apple Health, Google Connect, or CGMs with baseline outlier detection.

💊 Auto-Pharmacy Wallet

Predictive pill burn forecasting and dynamic auto-ordering via MedPlus, Tata 1mg, and Apollo Pharmacy.

Architecture & Tech Stack

Telephony & AI Voice

Twilio / Exotel PSTN voice gateway paired with ElevenLabs low-latency custom voice synthesis engine.

Conversational Memory

Fine-tuned LLM (Llama 3 / Claude 3.5) with Pinecone vector memory for long-term emotional context tracking.

Biometric Sync Engine

Terra API / Vital API integrating Apple Watch, Garmin, Fitbit, and TimescaleDB for time-series metrics.

E-Commerce & Wallets

Direct API webhooks with Tata 1mg, MedPlus, and Razorpay automated wallet debit integration.

Business Model & Monetization

B2C Subscription

$29 - $79 / month paid by family members for voice cloning, daily monitoring, and escalation alerts.

E-Pharmacy Margin

5% - 12% revenue share on automated refill orders generated for partner e-pharmacy platforms.

B2B2C Enterprise

Licensing to hospitals, chronic care clinics, and insurers to lower re-admission rates.

Compliance, Privacy & Guardrails

🔒 Data Protection & Privacy

Compliant with DPDP Act 2023, ABDM standards (India), and HIPAA guidelines for protected health information.

🎙️ Voice Clone Consent

Multi-factor verification and explicit authorization required before cloning any family member's voice.

🛡️ Medical Guardrails

Non-diagnostic assistant boundary. Prompts strictly prevent LLM from changing prescriptions or offering medical advice.

⚡ High Availability

Redundant PSTN carriers and fail-safe human tele-operator dispatch fallback.
